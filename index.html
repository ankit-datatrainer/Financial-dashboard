Research Playbook & System Architecture

Core Research Question

"Can AI identify deterioration or improvement in a company's credit quality earlier than traditional financial analysis by combining quantitative financial trends with what management says in MD&A reports and earnings calls?"

Triangulation Methodology

  1. Quantitative Trajectory: Deterministic calculation of growth, margins, interest coverage, and liquidity runway.
  2. Qualitative Narrative: Independent NLP scoring of Tone (0-3), Uncertainty (0-3), Specificity (0-3), and Q&A Defensiveness (0-3).
  3. Divergence Detection: Algorithmic comparison measuring whether management words align with audited reported accounting realities.

Time Contained Protocol

To eliminate hindsight bias, each historical quarter functions as a strictly frozen decision point. Predictions utilize only information filed on or before the historical cutoff date.
